01 / THE MATHEMATICS

A zero of M need not
be a zero of J.

For each fixed source Profile, every sufficiently small positive parameter admits a point strictly inside the first repair window where the mass history M vanishes and the angular-weighted history J is positive at that same point.

The parameter threshold depends on the Profile. The witness can depend on the parameter.

THE MECHANISM

Separated supports.
Unequal relative weights.

At a first-window mass zero, a positive second repair coefficient forces a negative first coefficient. The angular weight decreases across the two separated bumps, so mass balance cannot also cancel the angular history.

QUANTITATIVE EXTENSION

Jlocal ≥ K₁ · c₁ · I₀₁ · (u₀−λ − l₁−λ) > 0

A positive lower bound independent of the selected mass-zero witness, under the same Profile, exact mass-zero and positive-c₁ hypotheses. Ten quantitative and normalization propositions have a separate fixed-checkpoint Lean replay.
