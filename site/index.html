01 / RESEARCH CONTEXT

Begin with the equations.
Make the mathematics executable.

Navier–Stokes connects the evolution of incompressible velocity to transport, pressure, viscosity and forcing. It is the scientific setting for NSRW’s longer research program.

∂tu+(u·∇)u=−∇p+νΔu+f∇·u=0

u: velocity · p: pressure · ν: kinematic viscosity · f: forcing

The present study isolates the moment structure of one pinned construction. A source Profile fixes tail and schedule data, a coefficient bound and a consistent angular-reset witness. Its repair pulses enforce terminal history constraints.

02 / MATHEMATICAL FINDINGS

A zero of M need not
be a zero of J.

M is the source mass history. J is the corresponding H-weighted angular history. They accumulate the same underlying axial field with different weights.

For each fixed source Profile, every sufficiently small positive parameter admits a point strictly inside the first repair window where M = 0 and J > 0 at that same point.

The parameter threshold depends on the Profile; the witness may depend on the parameter.

WHY THIS RESULT MATTERS

Cancellation has structure.
The denominator matters.

The investigation began with a candidate normalized ratio, Q = J / (M H). At the point NSRW identifies, M vanishes while J stays positive. The result exposes a concrete denominator obstruction and explains it through the geometry of the repair pulses.

The contribution is the source-linked derivation, same-witness existence theorem and positive quantitative margin. Together they turn a question about cancellation into a precise target for certified computation.

THE PROOF MECHANISM

Separated supports.
Unequal relative weights.

At a first-window mass zero, a positive second repair coefficient forces a negative first coefficient. The angular weight decreases across the separated bumps, so mass balance leaves a strictly positive angular history.

QUANTITATIVE EXTENSION

Jlocal ≥ K₁ · c₁ · I₀₁ · (u₀−λ − l₁−λ) > 0

For the same Profile and parameter, every first-window mass zero with c₁ > 0 satisfies this bound. The right-hand side does not depend on which qualifying zero is selected.

Exact scope of this research snapshot

The Lean results concern fixed Profiles of the pinned outgoing construction, with Profile-dependent positive thresholds and first-window witnesses. The numerical results enclose core scalars. Numerical coefficient positivity, an explicit source Profile, a root bracket and comparative application utility are subsequent tasks. Independent external reproduction remains open. Uniqueness, first-zero ordering, ratio poles and a general Navier–Stokes solution are outside the present results.
