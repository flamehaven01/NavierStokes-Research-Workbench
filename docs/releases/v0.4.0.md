## Mathematical result

For each fixed pinned-source `Profile F`, sufficiently small positive `eta`
admits a radius strictly inside the first repair window at which the source
mass vanishes and its angular history is strictly positive **at the same point**.
The radius and positive neighborhood are existential, not numerical outputs.

Write `B=F.data.core.pulseStart`, `X_r=exp(B)*r`, and `l0,u0` for that
Profile's first repair bounds. The quantifier is
`forall F, exists epsilon_F>0, forall 0<eta<epsilon_F, exists l0<r<u0,
M_F(eta,X_r)=0 and J_F(eta,X_r)>0`; r may depend on eta.
The structural conditional theorem instead assumes interior-window M=0 and
actual second coefficient c1>0, **without eta>0**. Separated supports and the
decreasing relative tail weight force a strictly negative angular tail, hence
positive source J. Q=J/(M H) then fails its intended nonzero-denominator condition;
neither a pole/divergence nor an upstream theorem defect is established.

candidate contains 17 exact committed inputs, two post-execution receipt
snapshots, selected axiom lines, a bounded stage summary and the project license.

archive; its excerpts and hashes do not give access to every excluded raw byte.
